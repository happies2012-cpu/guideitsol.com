# Social Media Content System

## 1. Platform Strategy

### LinkedIn (Primary - B2B Growth)
- **Goal**: Establish Authority & Lead Gen.
- **Frequency**: 3x Weekly.
- **Content Mix**:
  - 40% Educational (How-to, Trends).
  - 30% Social Proof (Case Studies, Testimonials).
  - 20% Company Culture (Team, Behind the scenes).
  - 10% Hard Sell (Service promotion).

### Twitter/X (Community & Tech)
- **Goal**: Networking & Visibility.
- **Frequency**: Daily (Tweets + Replies).
- **Content**:
  - "Building in Public" updates.
  - Tech stack discussions (React, Node.js).
  - Sharing industry news.

### YouTube (SEO & Trust)
- **Goal**: Demonstrate Capability.
- **Frequency**: 2x Monthly.
- **Content**:
  - Demos of Flight Booking Engines.
  - Tutorials on Travel API Integration.
  - Client Success Stories.

## 2. Content Pillars

### Pillar A: Travel Technology
- Topics: GDS Integration, OTA features, White Label solutions.
- Audience: Travel Agency Owners, Startups.

### Pillar B: Enterprise Software
- Topics: Scalability, Cloud Security, Legacy Modernization.
- Audience: CTOs, IT Managers.

### Pillar C: Developer Hiring
- Topics: Remote teams, Indian talent, Cost-benefit analysis.
- Audience: Founders, Product Managers.
