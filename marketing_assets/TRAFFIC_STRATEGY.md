# Traffic Generation Strategy

## 1. Content Marketing (The Core Engine)
**Goal**: Drive organic traffic via high-value technical content.

### Blog Structure
- **Technical Tutorials**: "How to integrate Amadeus API", "Building Scalable React Apps".
- **Industry Insights**: "The Future of Travel Tech in 2025".
- **Case Studies**: Deep dives into client success.

### Content Calendar (Month 1)
| Week | Topic | Type | Keyword |
|------|-------|------|---------|
| 1 | Top 5 Flight Booking APIs | Listicle | Flight Booking API |
| 2 | Why Your Business Needs a Custom App | Educational | Custom App Development |
| 3 | React vs. Flutter for Startups | Comparison | React vs Flutter |
| 4 | GuideIT Solutions Case Study: Travel Portal | Case Study | Travel Portal Development |

## 2. Paid Acquisition (PPC)
**Google Ads**:
- Campaign 1: "Hire Dedicated Developers" (High intent, high CPC).
- Campaign 2: "Travel Technology Solutions" (Niche, lower CPC, high value).

**LinkedIn Ads**:
- Target CTOs and Product Managers in Travel & Hospitality sectors.

## 3. Social Media Distribution
- **LinkedIn**: Share technical snippets, success stories, and company culture.
- **Twitter/X**: Engage with dev communities, share "Building in Public" updates.
- **YouTube**: Short demos of the Travel Booking Engine features.

## 4. Email Marketing
- **Newsletter**: "The Tech Traveler" - Weekly updates on Travel Tech.
- **Drip Campaign**: For users who download the "Travel Portal Checklist" (Lead Magnet).

## 5. Partnerships
- Partner with Travel APIs (Amadeus, Sabre) to be listed as a certified developer.
- Guest post on tech blogs.
